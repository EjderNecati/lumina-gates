Our Story

A gate should belong
to the room it lives in.

Lumina Gates began with a simple frustration: every safety gate on the market looked like it belonged in a warehouse. We make the gate you would have chosen for your home if you weren't a parent or a pet owner — and then we engineer it to keep the smallest members of your household safe. Every piece is built to your exact opening, in our workshop, by hand.
